About us

Villa Food Products started serving its quality and hygienic products to our customers since 1970, as a manufacturer. We do, Supply and Export our produts such as wide varieties of Papad, Spices, Vathal, Vadam and all agricultural products in domestic as well as international markets. Our products are made from high-grade ingredients that guarantee their optimum quality. We are well reckoned as a prominent Manufacturer of high quality papad in India. The position that we have acquired is the result of the hard work of our highly efficient management team. A streamlined business activity, strict adherence with the quality & hygienic says about Villa in the market.

Quality Policy & Process

The quality of our products stands unrivaled in the market. We exercise stringent quality policies in all our business activities. To achieve the objective, we have our own in-house quality testing unit to check the quality of our products at every stage of production. For hygienic processing of our range, we have developed Processing unit, Packaging unit and Storage unit, which are ISO certified. Our food products are safe to use and are assured to be free from harmful preservatives or chemicals.
